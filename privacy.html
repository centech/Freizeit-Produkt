Stop Scrolling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026. Applies to Stop Scrolling for Android, package com.scrollstopper, version 7.0 onward.

Stop Scrolling is an offline digital wellbeing app from Freizeitprodukt (Maximilian Zinkewitz). Contact the developer about privacy at freizeitprodukt@gmail.com.

Accessibility access and its purpose

With your explicit in-app consent and Android accessibility permission, the AccessibilityService API supplies scroll distances and positions, the originating app's package identifier, and the view class that produced an event. We use these signals while you use other apps to count scrolling, apply your exclusions, show optional progress notifications, and open a break reminder when your chosen distance is reached.

The app does not retrieve screen contents, read messages, typed text or passwords, take screenshots, or perform gestures. It does not change settings or make decisions in other apps. The service is not designated as a disability accessibility tool.

Data stored on your device

  • Your threshold, inactivity and snooze settings, snooze end time, reminder display preference, and consent version.
  • The package identifiers of apps you choose to exclude.
  • The last 10 reminder records: time, originating app name and package identifier, and accumulated scroll distance. The distance can include scrolling in multiple apps during a session.
  • Totals for reminders, snoozes, and dismissals, and daily snooze and dismissal counts used for your statistics.

Live scroll positions and view classes are used in memory to estimate distance and are not saved in the reminder history.

Sharing, security, and backups

All app processing and storage happen on your device. The app has no Internet permission, accounts, advertising, analytics, payment, or tracking SDKs. It does not transmit or share your app usage information with the developer or third parties, and does not sell data.

Saved data is held in Android's private app storage. This version disables Android cloud backup and excludes app data from Android device transfer. Backups made by earlier versions may still exist in your backup provider's systems; manage those through that provider. Progress notifications are marked private for the lock screen, subject to your Android notification settings.

Retention and deletion

The reminder history retains at most 10 entries; new entries replace the oldest. Daily interaction entries older than 30 days are removed when that type of statistic is next updated. Lifetime totals, preferences, exclusions, and consent remain until you clear them or uninstall.

Use Clear reminder history and stats on the app's main screen to delete reminder records and interaction statistics. Settings and exclusions remain. New reminders create new records if monitoring is still active. To delete all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Stop Scrolling → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. No server account or server-side data exists to delete.

Your choices

You can decline the accessibility disclosure and still open the app. Monitoring stays paused until you agree. Use Withdraw consent and pause tracking to stop processing new events, or disable Stop Scrolling Watcher in Android Accessibility settings. Withdrawing consent does not erase past records; use the deletion controls above to do that. The updated disclosure requires consent again when upgrading from an older version.

Notification permission is optional and is used only to post scroll progress. Denying it does not enable or disable accessibility access. Break reminders can still appear with accessibility consent and permission.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or privacy rights, contact freizeitprodukt@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence outside the app. We use information you send only to respond and handle your request; do not include sensitive app history unless needed for your request. We cannot access or delete records held only on your device.

We will update this policy when app data handling changes and obtain new consent when required.
